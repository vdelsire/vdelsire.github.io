A Little About Me

Curious About How Things Work.
Driven To Make Them Better.

Hi, I’m Victor.

I’m a mechanical engineering student at the University of Michigan, working toward my Bachelor of Science in Engineering with an expected graduation in May 2027.

My projects span motorized linkages, competition robotics, solar vehicle systems, and a working hovercraft. I enjoy connecting the analytical side of engineering—CAD, simulation, and controls—with the practical work of machining, assembling, and testing.

In summer 2026, I researched wind turbine bearing dynamics at RWTH Aachen University in Germany. Earlier, I studied thermodynamics in Paris, exploring nuclear energy and the history of engineering. Those experiences have added an international perspective to the way I approach technical problems.

I’m based in Ann Arbor, Michigan. I also speak French at a professionally proficient level and conversational Spanish.
